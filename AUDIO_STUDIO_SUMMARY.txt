7) ROADMAP: VERY ADVANCED AUDIO (optimize + mix ONE OR MORE tracks)
================================================================================
This is the "what to do next" list to go from a good 2-song mashup to a
studio-grade engine that optimizes audio and mixes N tracks. Ordered by impact.
Each item = WHAT it does, the FREE/OPEN tool, and WHERE it plugs in.

--- A. TIGHTEN THE BEAT MATCH (biggest audible win) ---------------------------
[ ] A1. True downbeat grid, not first-beat.
        Tool: madmom (DBNDownBeatTrackingProcessor) or BeatNet.
        Where: audio_engine.detect_bpm_and_beats -> return downbeats; align on
        bar-ones. Fixes the "in tempo but slightly off" feel.
[ ] A2. Beat-synchronous stretch (warp to the grid), not one global ratio.
        Tool: pyrubberband per-beat, or rubberband --time-map.
        Where: mashup step 4 — stretch each bar to match, so tempo drift inside
        a song is corrected too.
[ ] A3. Better key detection for harmonic mixing.
        Tool: Essentia KeyExtractor (replaces the librosa Krumhansl profile).
        Where: audio_engine.detect_key.

--- B. STUDIO-GRADE MIX / MASTER (make it sound "released") -------------------
[ ] B1. Bass-swap EQ + multiband ducking (not just broadband duck).
        Tool: pedalboard (Spotify, VST-quality EQ/compressor/reverb) or scipy.
        Where: audio_engine.render_mashup — only one low-end at a time; duck the
        bed's mids under the vocal, keep its bass.
[ ] B2. Reference mastering to a pro track.
        Tool: Matchering 2.0 (open) — matches EQ + loudness to a reference song.
        Where: after the rating loop, before encode.
[ ] B3. De-ess / clean the extracted vocal (Demucs bleed).
        Tool: pedalboard de-esser + noisereduce.
        Where: after Demucs vocal stem, before mixing.
[ ] B4. True-peak limiting (no inter-sample clipping on phones).
        Tool: pyloudnorm + a simple look-ahead limiter, or ffmpeg alimiter.
        Where: audio_engine.normalize_loudness final stage.

--- C. SMARTER AI ARRANGEMENT ------------------------------------------------
[ ] C1. Labelled sections (intro/verse/chorus), not energy clusters.
        Tool: allin1 (All-In-One Music Structure Analyzer).
        Where: audio_engine.detect_sections -> feed real labels to Gemini so it
        drops the vocal chorus on the instrumental chorus exactly.
[ ] C2. Multi-section arrangement (verse over break, chorus over drop, etc.),
        with crossfades between placements — not one single vocal placement.
        Where: _arrange returns a LIST of {vocal_section, bed_section, action};
        render loops it using audio_engine.crossfade.
[ ] C3. Lyric/vocal-activity gating so vocals never sit on the bed's own vocals.
        Tool: Silero-VAD or the vocal stem's energy envelope.

--- D. MIX ONE OR MORE TRACKS (N-track engine) -------------------------------
[ ] D1. Generalize the request from 2 fixed keys to a LIST of sources:
        ProcessAudioRequest.sources = [{s3_key, role}], role in
        {vocal, bed, extra}. Downloader already handles any count.
        Where: main.py _mashup -> loop over sources; frontend: an "add another
        song" button (dynamic URL list) instead of two fixed fields.
[ ] D2. Auto role-detection: run Demucs on each, measure vocal vs. instrumental
        energy, and auto-pick who is the vocal and who is the bed.
[ ] D3. Common-grid warping: pick ONE target BPM + key for the whole set, warp
        every track to it (A1/A2), then layer/arrange all of them.
[ ] D4. N-track auto-gain / headroom management so 3-4 layers don't clip:
        loudness-match each stem, then sum with per-stem ducking priority
        (vocal > lead > pads).
[ ] D5. Stem mixer UI: per-track volume/mute/solo + which section each plays,
        so the user can nudge the AI's choices.

--- E. OPTIMIZE COST / SPEED / QUALITY KNOBS ---------------------------------
[ ] E1. Warm Demucs in-memory (get_model at @modal.enter) instead of the CLI
        subprocess, so N tracks don't each reload the model.
[ ] E2. Quality tiers: "fast" (htdemucs, 1 take) vs "best" (htdemucs_ft, 3-take
        rating loop + Matchering) — bill accordingly.
[ ] E3. Expand the rating loop to optimize more than PQ (also Content Enjoyment)
        and try a couple of arrangement candidates, not just gain back-off.
[ ] E4. Swap generate model to ACE-Step (Apache-2.0, commercial-safe) or YuE
        (full songs WITH sung vocals, Hindi/English) — see docs/14.

--- F. PRODUCT / LEGAL ------------------------------------------------------
[ ] F1. Copyright notice + private-only output policy for YouTube-sourced audio
        (product decision — flag before public launch).
[ ] F2. Audio player in the Clips grid (clips-grid.tsx) for mediaType "audio"
        (the one UI item intentionally left for you to approve).

Full technical rationale for A-C already written in docs/15 and docs/16.

9) REDESIGN: VIRAL HOOK EDIT (short "X × ABC", best-part, tune-focused)
================================================================================
Why: the old mashup exported the FULL song (263s / 306s), used the whole track
instead of the best part, and had thin effects. The mode was reworked into a
short, viral, best-part edit.

What changed (backend apps/mixer/):
- FULL extraction: Demucs htdemucs_ft now runs 4-stem (vocals / drums / bass /
  other), summed into one instrumental. Replaces the old --two-stems split.
  (main.py _demucs_four_stems)
- BEST PART: audio_engine.detect_hook() scores loudness + onset energy over
  sliding windows and returns the catchiest window(s) — the "best part."
- TUNE FOCUS: audio_engine.is_tune_only() drops the vocal and keeps just the
  instrumental tune when the vocal's energy share in the hook is low (a lyric
  that doesn't sit on the bed / a sparse section).
- TWO-PART "X × ABC": two sources → one hook each; a single source → its two
  best hooks. Parts are tempo + Camelot-key matched to one grid, then joined
  with audio_engine.beat_matched_transition() (high-pass sweep + crossfade).
- BANDLAB-STYLE FX: audio_engine.apply_fx_chain() runs a pedalboard channel
  strip (EQ / low-shelf warmth / glue compressor / air / light reverb /
  limiter). Falls back to a scipy high-pass + ceiling if pedalboard is missing,
  so FX never breaks the render. requirements.txt: added pedalboard, removed the
  now-unused faster-whisper.
- SHORT + AI-DECIDED LENGTH: audio_engine.decide_edit_length() picks the length
  musically from the tempo (~8-bar phrase per part) when the user leaves it on
  Auto; any explicit request is clamped to 8-59s. sequence_parts() enforces a
  hard MAX_VIRAL_SECONDS = 59 ceiling. No more long videos.
- Rating loop kept but simplified: heavy work (separation, warp) runs once; the
  loop only re-runs the cheap FX pass, trying "viral" then a cleaner "clean"
  preset and keeping the best Audiobox-PQ take.
- Removed dead code from the old overlay path: main._arrange,
  main._transcribe_vocal_phrases, main._clean_json, and engine helpers
  mix_vocal_over_bed, detect_sections, remix_cut, plan_arrangement_fallback,
  next_mix_params (+ their tests). Added CPU tests for all new helpers.

What changed (frontend):
- Audio Studio "AI mix" tab is now "Viral hook mix (X × ABC)"; default two
  sources are "Song A (X)" / "Song B (ABC)" on Auto roles.
- "Viral clip length" selector defaults to "Auto (AI decides)"; manual options
  are 15 / 20 / 30 / 45 / 59s only (no 90/120/180/full).
- createAdvancedMix: 0 = auto length; any number clamps to 8-59s. Inngest passes
  it straight through to the mixer (remix_duration_seconds; 0/None = auto).

10) GENRE COMPOSER + MULTIPLE CLIPS + REFERENCE MASTERING (open-source tools)
================================================================================
Driven by: "add beats + instruments like a real composer per genre", "handle
songs with/without lyrics", "generate multiple clips not just one", and "use
existing open-source software instead of building from scratch."

- Genre-aware composer (audio_engine.reproduce_bed + compose_genre_layer):
  detects the target style, then synthesizes and lays a matching kit on the beat
  grid — tabla+dholak for Bollywood, 808+hat-rolls for trap, four-on-the-floor
  for house, backbeat for pop/rock, mellow for lofi, pads for ambient — plus a
  sub-bass in the song's key and a triad instrument stab. Replaces the old
  generic kick/hat transform_bed_for_originality. Deterministic + CPU-tested.
- Lyric AND instrumental sources: is_tune_only + a has_vocals flag mean a source
  with no vocals becomes a tune-only part automatically; a lyric source keeps its
  vocal over the composed bed.
- Multiple clips per job (not one): the mixer now returns clips=[Remix, Flip,
  Chill]. Heavy work (Demucs, Rubber Band warp) runs once; each variation only
  re-runs the cheap composer + overlay + FX + sequence. Inngest create-audio-
  clips creates one Clip row per variation (db.clip.createMany); title carries
  the variation name. Credits unchanged (one job = 3 clips).
- Reference mastering with Matchering (github.com/sergree/matchering): the final
  master is EQ/RMS/peak/stereo-width matched to the source song's OWN original
  recording (already pro-mastered), so the mix sounds "released."
  audio_engine.reference_master() with a pyloudnorm fallback if it can't run.
  requirements.txt: added matchering, pedalboard already present.
- Deployed + tested (2026-07-13) on the two songs g0eO74UmRBs × bKZTnnFU9HA:
  3 clips, 33.8s each (auto length), hiphop composer kit (bollywood hip-hop
  fusion, 96 BPM, key -1st), master=matchering on all three, 0 clipping,
  ~-14.4 to -14.8 LUFS, Audiobox PQ 7.7-7.8. Variations verified distinct
  (pairwise mean|diff| 0.04-0.13).

NEXT (researched, not yet built): swap the synthesized composer kit for a real
open-source music model — ACE-Step 1.5 (github.com/ace-step/ACE-Step-1.5,
Apache-2.0, <4GB VRAM, <10s/song on GPU) to GENERATE a genre/BPM/key-matched
instrumental the vocal sits on. Bigger + GPU-heavier; vocal-over-generated-beat
alignment needs a listen-and-tune pass, so it's gated on a go-ahead.
